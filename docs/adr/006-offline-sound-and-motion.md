# ADR 006 — Offline sound effects and bounded motion graphics

Accepted 9 November 2026 under the owner's request for sound effects and more expressive animation. Implements PRD §5.3, FR-005/008/014/016/021 and AC-04/08/09/10 without changing gameplay rules, puzzle hashes, saves or rewards.

Use a small GameAudioGateway with a silent fake/default and an optional Flutter MethodChannel adapter. Android uses the OS SoundPool API (available before min API 26), iOS uses OS AVAudioPlayer/ambient AVAudioSession (available before min iOS 15). These are built-in system frameworks, not redistributed vendor SDKs. No new pub package, native plugin dependency, generator framework, analytics, audio recording, permission or network capability. Native adapter code is project-authored; there is no third-party package version/license to add. Alternatives such as a general audio plugin or Rive add dependencies/footprint without being required for short effects or vector bursts.

Eight original deterministic, mono 22.05 kHz PCM effects are generated by tools/build_audio.py and bundled. No third-party recordings or runtime synthesis/generation. Expected asset footprint below 200 KB; actual APK/AAB deltas and native test outcomes are recorded in BUILD_REPORT.md. Android caps concurrent voices at four; iOS keeps one prepared player per short cue. Decoded PCM is below 200 KB, excluding unmeasured system audio overhead.

Sound follows successfully committed changes; timer saves/opening sessions do not retrigger it. Swipes and Undo emit one cue per command, not per tile. Rejected garden placements use a soft warning; legal exploratory guesses are never judged against the solution. Audio failure must not block input, saving, progression or a win. Sound effects are independently switchable with persisted volume. Background/pause/mute stops current voices; no queued replay on resume. Android checks ringer/media-volume state and foreground activity. iOS ambient category respects the silent switch and mixes with other audio. No background music is introduced.

Motion remains native Flutter widgets/CustomPainter: placement bloom and region trace, short stroke/Undo ribbons, companion reactions, a bounded floating-island reveal and richer orbital/petal win flourish. No endless idle particles, flashes, camera shake or input-blocking animation. OS and app reduced-motion preferences disable decorative motion, and lifecycle/paused TickerMode gates stop it. Effects do not alter hit testing, semantic labels, region boundaries or the fitted layout.

Verify cue classification/mute/lifecycle/failure with fakes, bounded/reduced motion with widget tests, real SoundPool load/play/stop and native pause in an isolated Android package, existing gesture/tutorial/save/reward suites, release permissions and measured sizes. iOS remains unverified until a signed build and physical silent-switch/audio smoke test succeed.
