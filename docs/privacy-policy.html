Pawgrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
App: Pawgrid (Android package com.pawgrid.pawgrid)

Pawgrid (“the App”) is a fully offline garden logic puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and what it does not.

1. Summary

  • No account or login
  • No ads, analytics, crash SDKs, or tracking in the shipping App
  • No internet connection required to play
  • Game progress and settings stay on your device
  • We do not sell personal information

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores gameplay data locally on your device so you can save and resume play. This may include puzzle progress, settings (such as sound, motion, and accessibility preferences), rewards/ledger state, and tutorial completion status.

This information stays on your device. The App does not upload it to our servers because the shipping App has no gameplay backend and does not require network access.

3. Information we do not collect

The shipping App does not collect or request:

  • Name, email, phone number, or account credentials
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or files beyond local app storage
  • Advertising ID or tracking identifiers for advertising
  • Analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting data through third-party SDKs

4. Internet and third parties

The release build is designed to work offline and does not include ads, purchases, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs that would send data to third parties for those purposes.

Google Play or the Apple App Store may process information related to downloading, updating, or reviewing the App under their own policies. That processing is controlled by the store provider, not by Pawgrid gameplay servers.

This privacy-policy webpage is hosted separately from the App. When you visit it, the hosting provider may process standard web-request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the page. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or tracking cookies to this page. Visiting this page does not give the website access to your local game saves.

5. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App stores progress only on-device and does not operate an account system, there is no personal profile for us to collect from a child through the App.

6. Data deletion

Local progress and preferences remain until you delete them. You can remove them using Settings → “Delete all local data” within the App, by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the App. This removes your local progress, rewards, companions and preferences. The App does not provide cloud backup or cross-device sync. Because we do not maintain a cloud account for the App, there is no remote player profile for us to delete on a server.

7. Support and privacy inquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and resolve support or privacy requests. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us below. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information or game data you do not want to share.

8. Storage and security

Game data is kept in the App’s local storage, protected by your device’s operating-system controls. We do not operate a server copy of that data. Access to an unlocked or compromised device may expose local information; no storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. If future versions introduce online features or different data practices, we will update the policy to describe those practices.
